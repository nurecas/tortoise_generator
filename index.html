Fractal structure

Field

The structure as a continuous glowing field. The Tortoise is the Hopf fibration, so its fibres are field lines of a known field that fills space: the drawn fibres flare as spikes, and the torus body glows with the fibres of the smaller tori nested inside it. Everything moves with the counter-rotation. The main torus and its first copies glow; smaller copies appear when the field glows over the structure.

Phenomenology

The same structure, seen as bodies. The fibres, the first-level nodes and the bridge each become a figure from one of the world's visions: arms, wings, serpents or fire round the torus, faces, eyes or treasures in its gaps, a body, a tree or a mountain through its hole. The geometry underneath does not change.

Display & geometry

Animation

The two chirality families counter-rotate by α(t). Rotation about a node's local z is exactly a phase shift on this parametrisation, but it slides the interstitial sites along a meridian — so the whole tree is re-solved each frame and the children follow their gaps.
